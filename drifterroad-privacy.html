Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Olympus Games ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the mobile game Drifter Road ("the Game").

We may collect certain types of information to provide and improve the Game, including:

The Game displays ads provided by third-party advertising networks. These partners may collect and use personal data (such as your advertising ID) to show personalized ads. Examples of such partners may include Google AdMob and Unity Ads.

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used to:

The Game is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us so we can delete it.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. However, we may share anonymized or aggregated data with analytics or ad partners to improve the Game and deliver ads.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Your Choices

You can limit the collection of personal data by adjusting your device settings, such as resetting your advertising ID or limiting ad tracking.
