Account Deletion

Assembly Line 2 is developed and published by Olympus.

This game uses Unity Authentication, a service provided by Unity Technologies, to manage player accounts.

How to Delete Your Account

To delete your account used in Assembly Line 2, you must delete your Unity account. Please note that this action is permanent and will remove your account across all Unity-supported games and services.

Delete Your Account

Data Deletion

When your Unity account is deleted, your personal data associated with Assembly Line 2 will also be deleted or anonymized in accordance with our data retention practices.
